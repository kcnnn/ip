How to document a brittle test · Safety & steps

Only a qualified person with safe roof access, fall protection, and property-owner authorization should manipulate shingles. Testing can damage roofing. Follow the specific shingle manufacturer’s repair instructions; this checklist is not a standardized test.

  1. Choose and record the slope and test location. Photograph the shingle before touching it, noting any existing cracks or creases.
  2. Record air and roof-surface temperature and weather. Defer if the roof is unsafe, wet, icy, or shingles are cold/stiff. Temperature affects handling.
  3. During an authorized repair assessment, gently lift only as much as needed for the manufacturer’s repair procedure. Do not force a sealed tab, fold it back, or bend to an arbitrary angle. Stop at resistance or the first sign of cracking, tearing, or creasing.
  4. Photograph and describe the result. Distinguish pre-existing damage from any change during handling. Record “inconclusive” or “not performed” when appropriate.
  5. Have the qualified roofer restore, reseal, or replace disturbed material according to the manufacturer’s instructions. Do not leave the roof exposed.

Temperature reference: Owens Corning cold-weather handling guidance (not a brittle-test standard).
