How to document a brittle test · Safety & steps
  1. Choose and record the slope and test location. Photograph the shingle before touching it, noting any existing cracks or creases.
  2. Record air temperature and weather. Defer if the roof is unsafe, wet, icy, or shingles are cold/stiff. Temperature affects handling.
  3. During an authorized repair assessment, gently lift only as much as needed for the manufacturer’s repair procedure. Do not force a sealed tab, fold it back, or bend to an arbitrary angle. Stop at resistance or the first sign of cracking, tearing, or creasing.
  4. Take a photo during the gentle lift, then another after release. Describe any visible cracks or creases, distinguishing existing damage from changes during handling. Record “inconclusive” or “not performed” when appropriate.
  5. Restore, reseal, or replace disturbed material according to the manufacturer’s instructions. Do not leave the roof exposed.
